Behind The Counter

We built this for our neighbours.

Two years ago, in an old picture-framing shop on Chalk Lane, Fig & Filter opened its doors. Named after the mature fig tree shading our courtyard and the daily craft of filter coffee, we wanted a place that roasts its own beans and bakes everything from scratch right here.

park The 40-year-old courtyard fig tree at No. 8 Chalk Lane
