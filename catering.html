Good to know

What if we have vegan, gluten-free, or dairy-free guests?➜

We clearly label all individual boxes. Gluten-free and vegan pastries are baked in a dedicated tray each morning. Just note your dietary numbers in your email.

Do we need to return the coffee carafes?➜

Yes, our thermal carafes stay piping hot for 4 hours. We arrange an afternoon pickup between 2:00pm and 4:30pm, or you can drop them back to 8 Chalk Lane next morning.

Can we order for small teams under 6 people?➜

Our delivery packages have a 6-person minimum to cover courier logistics. For smaller groups, you are welcome to pick up boxes directly at the café counter via our regular café menu.
